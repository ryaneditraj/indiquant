# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Sonar (a Money Radar product by Thugir Group) a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

**Positive:** evidence-focused discussion, graceful disagreement, empathy.

**Unacceptable:** harassment, publishing private data, promoting guaranteed trading profits/scams, encouraging commercial misuse of this non-commercial license.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove contributions or ban participants for violations.
