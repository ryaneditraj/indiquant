# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Indiquant a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Indiquant is a Money Radar product by Thugir Group. The repository is maintained by Ryan Editraj.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Discussing evidence, methods, and results with precision
- Accepting disagreement about a hypothesis without personal attacks
- Giving and receiving feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the people using this research OS

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Promoting guaranteed trading profits, scams, or fabricated performance
- Encouraging commercial use that violates the PolyForm Noncommercial license

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may ban participants for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when someone is representing Indiquant or its community.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to the maintainer, [Ryan Editraj](https://github.com/ryaneditraj), via a private GitHub message or by emailing the address on the git history. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports and respond with what action they will take.
